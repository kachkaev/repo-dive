Let legend items hide series in the lines-of-code, dependency and loose-ends charts.
Click a legend item to take its series out of the stack (the item stays in place, crossed out) and click again to bring it back; alt/option-click an item to show only that series, or to show everything again when it is already the only one.
The axes stay put and the hover card keeps reporting every series, so hiding a category that spiked lets the slopes of the others be read against the same scale.
